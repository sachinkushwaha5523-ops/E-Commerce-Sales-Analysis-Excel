# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Performance Analysis Using Excel

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to identify key business trends, profitability, customer behavior, regional performance, product category performance, payment preferences, and return patterns.

The objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful **business insights and interactive visualizations** that can support data-driven decision-making.

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

### 🏆 Product Category Performance

**Electronics** was the highest revenue-generating category with approximately **₹33.19 Lakh** in revenue, making it the strongest contributor to overall sales.

### 🌎 Regional Performance

The **South region** was the top-performing region with approximately **₹12.98 Lakh** in revenue.

### 🔄 Return Analysis

**Fashion** recorded the highest return rate at approximately **8.3%**, indicating a potential area for investigation related to product quality, sizing, customer expectations, or product descriptions.

### ⚠️ Profitability Analysis

**Grocery** generated approximately **₹82K revenue** but recorded a negative profit of approximately **₹9.2K**, highlighting a potential issue related to pricing, discounts, product costs, or margins.

### 💳 Payment Method Performance

**Credit Card** was the most frequently used payment method and generated approximately **₹20.57 Lakh** in revenue.

### 📅 Monthly Performance

Monthly revenue trends were analyzed to identify high- and low-performing periods and understand changes in sales performance throughout the year.
